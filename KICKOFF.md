# Mission 001-R: find an overlooked route to first revenue

Read AGENTS.md, research/SCORING.md, state/STATUS.md and state/ledger.json. Record the actual trial start date only when the owner launches this mission. Use no paid resources or subagents.

Investigate up to six distinct opportunities across at least three commercial categories, with no more than eight public research queries. Consider lawful arbitrage (including fees, returns and fulfillment), service/product packaging, underserved professional niches, legally reusable data products, digital goods and distribution opportunities. These are examples, not required selections. Think beyond them. Reject any approach needing unapproved funds, buying inventory, debt, unapproved accounts or inaccessible tools; record those as future ideas only. Do not rely on speculative trading or gambling.

Prioritise paths with plausible repeat monthly purchases or recurring revenue, without disqualifying a credible fast first sale. Explain how the strongest opportunity could progress towards GBP 100 monthly net profit and ultimately self-fund AI capacity; label projections as scenarios, not realised results. Do not purchase or upgrade anything.

For each candidate, look for dated evidence of actual willingness to pay, competitor prices, reachable customer channels and overlooked advantages. Verify relevant platform and legal constraints from authoritative public sources when material. Cite links and dates. Separate observed facts, hypotheses and unknowns. If browsing is unavailable, do not invent evidence; report the blocker and a bounded research plan.

Write research/001-opportunities.md: up to six candidates, explicit gate decisions, scored shortlist of up to three eligible options, sources, risks and reasons for rejection. Each shortlisted option must specify customer, problem, offer, possible price, estimated unit economics including fees, distribution channel, expected time and manual work, repeatability, and a falsifiable test. Do not force a shortlist if fewer candidates qualify. If none qualify, recommend no-go.

If a candidate qualifies, choose one and write experiments/001-proposal.md. Specify the smallest credible willingness-to-pay test, exact deliverable, hypothesis, success/failure metrics, £0 incremental cash budget, max effort/session count, required approvals (scope, cost, platform or recipient and risks), and a human review point. No customer contact, publishing, deployment, purchases, account creation, payment collection or complete product build in this mission. If no candidate qualifies, write a no-go proposal instead.

Update state/STATUS.md and state/SELF-FUNDING.md, preserve the ledger (no fabricated revenue or expenses), and write a dated report in reports/. Finish by recommending one next action or a no-go, flag any uncertainties, and stop for the investor's decision.
