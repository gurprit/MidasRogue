# Mission 005-R: Activate the portfolio

Investor decision: end the four-mission discovery cycle and adopt v0.3 experimental capitalism. The owner has approved a narrow read-only commercial assessment of PrismShift, Offlink, TakeawayKit, Dungeon Deck and Milni, subject to AGENTS.md. No other project edits, publishing, outreach, spending or account access are approved.

Goal: prepare ONE small, reversible commercial validation experiment that is ready for the investor to approve and run. Do not write another broad speculative market report. Do not reopen tender triage or the Squarespace listing. Do not assume these products are currently released or have paying customers.

## One session
- Budget GBP 0 additional cash, 30 minutes active effort maximum, no subagents. Use no more than one third of effort on discovery. Record elapsed time and actual usage if visible, otherwise unknown.
- Review the existing assets via public listings, product pages and accessible non-sensitive owner-authorised documentation only. Ask for aggregate analytics only if necessary; do not access dashboards, private customer data or secrets. Do not copy proprietary material or private metrics into this potentially public repo.
- Rank up to three options, prioritising the shortest path to a real audience and use of existing work. An existing store listing or historic organic usage may be a useful signal, not proof of future sales. A product not yet released may need a different test; do not bypass release gates.
- Select one test using research/SCORING.md. State the precise audience, offer, proposed price or call to action, channel, evidence, hypothesis, metric, success/failure threshold, duration, estimated fees/contribution and support effort. No requirement to prove buyers will pay before testing.
- Prepare the actual test materials inside MidasRogue: e.g. one truthful store listing revision, a draft launch post for a named permitted community, or a minimal local offer page. Do not publish or alter any other project. Verify claims and rights; do not promise unavailable functionality. Prefer work that takes the owner less than 15 minutes to approve or execute.
- If no safe test can be prepared, identify the single missing input or permission that would unblock the strongest option. Do not start another broad search.

## Deliverables
Create experiments/005-portfolio/PLAN.md and the launch-ready draft artifact(s) alongside it, a short investor report, and update state/STATUS.md and state/SELF-FUNDING.md. Preserve the ledger and all prior research. End with a single precise approval request (channel, content, scope, timing, cost and risks). Do not merge the PR. No external execution is authorised by this mission.

The original trial began 20 November 2026; the £100 monthly profit review goal, earned cash reserve rule and all lawful/honest conduct and cash controls remain unchanged.
