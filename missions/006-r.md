# Mission 006-R: First paying Milni customers

Investor direction: pursue a real paid launch rather than use Product Hunt solely for hypothetical price responses. The original 005-R plan is postponed, not executed. Proposed offer: free organiser draft and guest access, £19 one-time activation for new weddings, existing weddings grandfathered. No guarantee of sales. A separate Codex task in the Milni workspace will implement and test checkout; do not modify Milni from this repository.

## One bounded session
GBP 0 additional cash, 30 minutes maximum active effort, no subagents. Use at most one third for discovery. Prepare one concrete acquisition experiment, not another broad market report.

Focus first on UK couples organising multi-event Indian weddings and wedding planners/coordinators who already serve them. Verify named relevant communities with promotional rules or planner partnerships with a permitted approach. Prioritise opt-in introductions through existing contacts, admin-approved helpful community posts and relevant supplier partnerships over Product Hunt. Consider Product Hunt as a supplementary launch once paid onboarding is ready; do not automatically publish or ask for votes.

Identify up to three specific reachable channels, with source links, access constraints, target audience, intended message and owner effort. Select one primary channel. Prepare one truthful, ready-to-use demo/pitch and short conversion-focused copy, clearly describing draft versus paid activation. Do not claim paid functionality is live until verified. No fabricated testimonials, fake scarcity, scraping personal data, spam or unapproved contact. For UK marketing comply with PECR and UK GDPR: public contact information alone is not consent; individual subscribers including sole traders generally require consent or applicable soft opt-in for electronic marketing. Verify current ICO rules and platform requirements. Ask for specific approval before any outreach or publication. No personal details in repo.

Propose a 14-day launch test after readiness: aim for 10 qualified organiser conversations or opt-in demo visitors and 1 genuine paid activation as the first milestone, with 3 paid activations a stretch goal. These are experiment goals, not predictions. Record aggregate qualified visitors, draft creations, checkout starts, confirmed paid activations, refunds and fees, with source attribution where lawful and privacy-preserving. Low reach is a distribution failure, not proof of low demand. Measure sales, not upvotes. Do not count test-mode payments. Close or revise after the observation period; no automatic further activity.

## Release gates
Before paid launch confirm server-enforced wedding activation/invitations, backwards compatibility of existing weddings, test-mode payment and refund handling, production configuration by owner, verified canonical URL, safe demo, clear customer terms including access/storage duration, applicable cancellation/refund rules and privacy, hosting capacity and cost limits, and explicit investor approval. Do not promise indefinite hosting or enable live checkout during this mission. If a necessary gate is unresolved, identify it and prepare materials only.

Write experiments/006-milni-acquisition/PLAN.md and the selected launch materials, update state/STATUS.md, state/SELF-FUNDING.md and a brief report. Preserve actual ledger at £0 unless verified transactions arrive. Record time/usage if known. Return one precise approval request specifying audience, channel, content, cost and timing. Keep draft PR #1 unmerged.
