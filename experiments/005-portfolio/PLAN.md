# Mission 005-R · Milni price-signal experiment

**Decision.** Prepare one Product Hunt launch for Milni and ask a specific one-time price question. This is a reversible commercial test at the offer level. The public page and comments may persist after the observation window.

**Portfolio review.** Reviewed authorised product documentation for all five named products and inspected Milni’s public landing and empty creation page without signing in or entering data. Milni was the only candidate in this review with a verified public organiser entry point suitable for the planned post. Release readiness or audience access sufficient for this test was not established for the other four, so no alternative was scored or prepared. [Milni landing page](https://milne.app/)

**Audience and evidence.** Indian wedding couples and organisers who come across Milni on Product Hunt while looking for planning tools. Product Hunt search surfaced wedding planners and templates, offering a plausible, broad category route. Relevant reach is unknown. Joy advertises free sites with RSVP and travel tooling, so the case for payment depends on specific value from multi-event logistics and traditions. These are product and category signals. No past Milni sales or traffic were assumed. [Product Hunt wedding search](https://www.producthunt.com/search?q=wedding%20planning) · [Joy](https://withjoy.com/wedding-website/)

**Scoring.** Reachable audience 2/5 × 30, cost/speed 4/5 × 25, problem plausibility 3/5 × 20, unit economics 3/5 × 15, repeatability 3/5 × 10. Total 59/100. The highest uncertainty is how many relevant organisers the launch reaches. Scope uses the existing product, free submission and manual aggregate counting. External gates are owner authority, an eligible existing personal account, accurate listing fields, permission to use the existing public onboarding link and confirmation that incremental launch traffic creates no charges.

## Execution after approval

1. The owner checks the existing personal Product Hunt account can submit and confirms the linked Milni onboarding is intended for public use and cannot create incremental charges. Confirm product name, target URL and current claims before scheduling. If any requirement fails, hold. The Product Hunt launch itself is free and requires a personal account with posting access. [Submission rules](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/479557-how-to-post-a-product) · [Launch FAQ](https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17734646-8-launch-faq-and-myths)
2. Publish one listing using [LISTING.md](LISTING.md), the thumbnail and gallery image, and the first comment. No paid promotion, additional posts or solicitations for votes. Use Product Hunt comments only for the test. Do not introduce checkout, change pricing or promise delivery of a paid plan. Allow one short batch of factual replies using the draft guidance.
3. Evaluate seven days after publication. Count distinct commenters who self-identify as planning or organising an Indian wedding and provide a clear answer or condition. Record only aggregate counts of yes, no and conditional, without handles, dates, locations or quotations that identify people. Do not count likes, generic praise or signups as price acceptance. This experiment does not require analytics or additional data collection.
4. **Hypothesis and stop rule.** At least two eligible commenters say they would pay £19 once, out of at least five eligible price responses. Success merits a separate paid-test decision. At least five responses with fewer than two affirmative answers fails the price signal. Fewer than five responses is inconclusive on price and fails this acquisition test. Stop at day seven in all cases. No automatic extension, outreach or offer changes.

**Effort and operational limits.** Target under 15 minutes of owner time across submission and the final count, with one optional reply batch of up to five additional minutes. No new support or service promises. If the release needs any product change, a new account, paid service or sensitive data access, hold and bring the issue back for a decision.

**Cash and repeatability.** Test price is hypothetical. £0 incremental spend, £0 fees and £0 expected receipts during the test. For later planning only, a £19 one-time sale charged via standard UK cards at Stripe’s published 1.5% plus 20p would leave about £18.51 before tax, hosting, refunds or support. A £2 per-sale operating/refund allowance leaves £16.51. Seven sales per month would contribute about £115.57 under that scenario. These are repeat sales to new organisers, with no assumed subscriptions. Payment setup, taxes, consumer terms, infrastructure costs and support capacity would need separate validation and approval before a paid test. [Stripe UK pricing](https://stripe.com/gb/pricing)

**Approval requested.** Approve one owner-posted Milni listing and its maker comment on Product Hunt for a seven-day £19 price-signal test at £0, subject to the existing-account and zero-cost checks above. The public association and comments may persist. Possible risks include irrelevant traffic, public questions and increased use of the existing onboarding flow. No payment collection is part of the proposal.
