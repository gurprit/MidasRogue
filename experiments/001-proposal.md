# Mission 001-R decision

**No-go for a commercial experiment.** The best lead is weekly bid triage for small UK facilities-maintenance suppliers. Contracts Finder gives those suppliers a free starting point. The proposed value is a faster bid-or-pass decision, and there is no verified paid demand or free, permitted route to a buyer for that offer.

If the investor approves one follow-up, spend up to 30 minutes in one session and use at most three public lookups to verify a named channel's rules and find one dated buyer request or priced comparable for bid qualification. Cash budget £0. Return the source links, channel rules and one example of what a supplier receives for the listed price or requests from a provider. Stop if either the route or demand evidence is missing. Check for registration fees and rules on solicitation and reuse. No contact, posting, account creation, payment collection or product build during this follow-up.

If both checks pass, return for approval of the exact channel, recipient, terms, cost and risks before any solicitation. The proposed later test would offer one manual one-page bid-or-pass brief for £10, based on one public notice and buyer-supplied eligibility criteria they may share. The hypothesis is that at least one of three qualified prospects would pay £10 for the brief within seven days and express interest in a weekly version. Zero purchases would fail the price test. A purchase would validate one transaction, with repeat demand still to be measured. Any later trial requires verified fees and a permitted payment route with no upfront spending.
