No candidate cleared the feasibility and compliance gates. No weighted shortlist was scored. Tender triage is the first verification priority because the free primary source already supplies notices and award history, and the proposed deliverable can use a single public notice. Award history says nothing about what suppliers would pay for triage.

## Tender triage scenario

A small facilities-maintenance supplier could buy a weekly brief for **£25 per month** as a pricing hypothesis. Deliver one page each week with up to three matching live notices, mandatory requirements, deadlines, links and an explicit pass reason where appropriate. Model £0 fixed cash costs and, as a sensitivity assumption, 5% combined payment costs and refunds plus £1 per account monthly in other cash costs. That gives £22.75 per account before tax. Five retained accounts would yield £113.75 monthly under those assumptions. Actual fees, refunds, time and retention are unknown. At an illustrative 20 minutes per account per week, five accounts take about 7.2 hours in an average month, before acquisition and administration. Track labour separately and check whether a shared search genuinely saves time. The £100 result would trigger an affordability review under the charter. Any upgrade would also depend on recurring realised cash, the actual subscription price and the two-month earned reserve.
