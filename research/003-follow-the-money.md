At an illustrative US$1.32 per £1 that is about £43.18, with actual exchange and payout charges unknown. A two- to three-hour agent estimate plus 30 minutes of human work yields roughly £12–£17/hour before acquisition and any revisions. An additional 15 minutes on the first application reduces that to roughly £12–£16/hour if won on this bid. Lost pitches reduce the effective rate further. The buyer's approved brief and template are unavailable before selection. A section rebuild or wider mobile defects could make this unprofitable. Skip if the template entails a redesign, delivery needs more than two sessions, cash must be paid up front or access cannot be restricted. Repeat maintenance is possible but the listing promises no follow-up.
