# Opportunity scoring

## Gate before scoring
An eligible candidate must have a plausible £0 incremental cash route to test, a permitted and realistically reachable distribution channel, a deliverable the agent and owner could fulfill, and no known unlawful, deceptive, exploitative or harmful element. Material unresolved compliance or cost questions prevent execution; score as provisional or defer, with a specific verification action. A high score never overrides a gate or approval requirement.

## Weighted comparison
Score each criterion from 0 (no supporting evidence or very poor) to 5 (strong evidence or very favourable). For uncertainty use a conservative score and explain it. Multiply each score by its weight divided by five; sum to a maximum of 100. Scores are prioritisation estimates, not probabilities.

| Criterion | Weight | What a high score means |
|---|---:|---|
| Evidence of willingness to pay | 25 | Traceable purchases, explicit paid demand or strong priced alternatives |
| Speed to first revenue | 25 | Short realistic path to a verified payment |
| Customer access | 20 | Permitted, reachable channel with credible acquisition economics |
| Unit economics | 15 | Positive estimated margin after fees, refunds and fulfilment |
| Repeatability | 10 | Potential to repeat without proportional manual effort |
| Low execution burden | 5 | Small test, few dependencies, modest owner time |

Report gate status, raw scores, weighted total, evidence quality, unknowns and the reason for the final recommendation. Prefer direct demand signals to search volume. For net cash profit use only confirmed receipts and expenses in the ledger; estimates are separate. Track owner time and allocated AI costs separately to understand economic viability.
