# Opportunity scoring v0.3

## Non-negotiable gates
The proposed test must have a plausible GBP 0 incremental cash route, a named permitted distribution channel with a credible audience, lawful rights and access, an achievable deliverable, bounded support and effort, and no known deception, exploitation or harm. Unresolved compliance, permissions or costs block the affected external action. Approval is required before publishing, pricing changes, contacting buyers, deployment, contracts or money movement. A high score never overrides these gates.

Unknown demand, conversion or retention are NOT disqualifiers: a test exists to measure them. Category-level commercial evidence, an existing audience or a concrete user problem can justify a small test; distinguish each from proof of sales. Do not manufacture a customer signal.

## Weighted comparison
Score 0–5 per criterion; weighted total = sum(score × weight / 5). These are estimates, not probabilities.

| Criterion | Weight | Strong evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Reachable audience and distribution | 30 | Named permitted channel and credible access |
| Speed and cost of a meaningful test | 25 | Reversible, no cash outlay, launch-ready quickly |
| Problem and demand plausibility | 20 | Specific use case, customer signal or paid comparable |
| Estimated unit economics | 15 | Positive contribution after fees and support |
| Repeatability and low owner effort | 10 | Same asset can sell repeatedly |

Rank at most three viable options. Select one; record the key unknown, test metric and threshold, effort cap, fees, support burden and exact approval required. Do not score unsupported facts as established. Stop if the gates fail, not merely because sales have not happened. Track realised receipts separately from projections and owner/AI time.
