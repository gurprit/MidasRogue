# Mission 004-R · Unconventional entrepreneurship

Observed 20 November 2026. Screened repeat-sale assets, automated data services, licensing, affiliate distribution and incentives. The £0 constraint rules out inventory, advertising spend and paid platform access. A viable experiment also needs a specific paying use case, a reachable first buyer and a bounded support obligation.

## What the previous missions established

| Mission | Evidence and decision |
| --- | --- |
| 001-R | Six ideas screened. Tender triage had a plausible data source but no verified buyer route. |
| 002-R | Buyer listings showed demand for domain-specific sourcing bundled with bid delivery. Low-cost alert tools already offered qualification context. The standalone service was closed. |
| 003-R | A US$75 Squarespace repair had 52 proposals and unverified eligibility and experience. The investor passed on it in the absence of that evidence, and no application was authorised. |

The common failure was acquisition. A listing or data source establishes availability. A repeat-sale experiment needs a reason that buyers in a reachable channel would choose the specific product.

## Economics and decision

Payhip's free tier charges 5% before processing. For the £6 pack, assume a further 3% plus £0.25 per order for processing and £0.25 per sale for incidental costs and refunds. Modelled contribution would be £5.02 before owner time and tax, or £100.40 from 20 sales in a month. This is a sensitivity case. Actual processor terms, refund rates and acquisition are unresolved. Gumroad charges 10% plus US$0.50 for direct sales and 30% via Discover, which requires at least US$100 in genuine sales and review before eligibility. Neither supplies a verified first-sale route for the pack. [Payhip pricing](https://payhip.com/pricing) · [Gumroad pricing](https://gumroad.com/pricing) · [Discover eligibility](https://gumroad.com/help/article/79-gumroad-discover)

**Stop active discovery.** No experiment cleared the combination of paid use case, reachable first buyer and acceptable recurring effort. Superhive demonstrates that an asset can sell repeatedly through a relevant marketplace. It does not establish demand for this proposed checker. No build or launch is recommended. Reopen only when new concrete evidence arrives, such as a paying buyer with an unmet workflow need or permission to evaluate an existing asset together with its demonstrably reachable audience. Tender triage stays closed.
