## Channel and unit economics

PeoplePerHour says there is no registration fee or fee to post an Offer. Its rules call for a specific fixed-price deliverable, delivery within five days of receiving requirements and disclosure when AI primarily produces the deliverable. Actual seller acceptance, paid acquisition costs and access to repeat orders remain unverified. No account was created. [Fees](https://support.peopleperhour.com/hc/en-us/articles/205218337-Freelancer-commission-fees) · [Offer rules](https://support.peopleperhour.com/hc/en-us/articles/205218167-Offers-Policies)

Base model for a new UK buyer: 20% platform commission under £250 lifetime billing per buyer, plus assumed 20% VAT on that fee without VAT recovery, or 24% withheld in total. Model £1 per billed unit as a cash contingency for incidental costs and no paid promotion or credits. This is a scenario, not an actual invoice. Cash contribution is before tax and owner time. No refunds, payout or FX costs modelled in the table. Verify charges before any launch.

| Work sold | Cash contribution | Time assumption | Effective contribution per hour |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| £25 monthly triage | £18.00 per account/month | 20 min/week plus 15 min/month admin, about 102 min/month | £10.62 before acquisition |
| £30 one-off brief | £21.80 | 75 min checking, writing and admin plus 120 min acquisition | £6.71 first sale |
| £49 one-off brief | £36.24 | Same 195 min first sale | £11.15 first sale, £28.99 on a repeat without acquisition time |

Acquisition base case assumes one sale from ten suitable pitches of 12 minutes each. This is an untested conversion assumption, and £0 cash acquisition assumes free platform access and enough eligible requests. Five or twenty pitches give £16.11 or £6.90/hour at £49. At an illustrative £15/hour owner-time hurdle, first-sale acquisition alone costs £30 in time, and fulfilment costs £18.75. The first-sale break-even price is about £65.46 including the £1 cash contingency. For triage, six retained accounts contribute £108/month before tax and require roughly 10.2 fulfilment hours/month, plus acquisition and replacement of churned customers. Three £49 orders per month contribute £108.72, with no basis to count those orders as recurring. Neither scenario is realised profit or earned reserves.

## Decision and different direction

**Close tender triage.** The observed buyer scope requires specialist expertise and often submission support. The narrow service faces strong substitutes and weak first-sale economics at plausible test prices. No sample or offer was prepared.

Next direction for investor consideration: buyer-led spreadsheet reconciliation and QA for non-sensitive operational exports, sold as a bounded exception report with acceptance checks. A buyer's Excel reconciliation request showed interest in recurring work, but its advertised price was a placeholder, it had 79 proposals and the underlying bank statements are outside the proposed scope. Use it as a discovery signal only. A future research session should start from current buyer postings that specify sample inputs, a bounded check and a usable budget. Require a permitted route, synthetic or non-sensitive inputs and a price that covers fees, QA and acquisition time before drafting a test.
